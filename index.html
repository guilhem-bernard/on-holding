01 · Growth

Sales growth compared with peers

At constant currency in FY2025, On grew almost twice as fast as Asics and four times as fast as Deckers. Asia-Pacific (+106.7%) and direct sales (+39.9%) led. The strong franc took 5.6 points off reported growth, which was 30.0%.

02 · Margins

Margins compared with peers

On has the best gross margin of the three and the thinnest operating margin, because half of its sales go to SG&A. SG&A rose from 49.1% of sales in FY2022 to 50.3% in FY2025. Asics shows the leverage exists: its SG&A fell from 42.5% to 39.2% in two years.

03 · Guidance

04 · Outlook

Outlook to 2029: three scenarios

My one disagreement with management is on costs. I trust the sales and gross margin targets, beaten every year, and half of the cost target. Result: 20.9% in 2029.

At the 22 September 2026 price, On traded at 14.4x EV/EBIT, the same as Asics (Deckers 7.5x), and its enterprise value was 8.3 times my 2029 EBIT. This is not a price target or investment advice.

I structured the project and set its goals, then made the calls: the company, the angle, the method, the credibility verdict, the scenarios and the prognosis. I steered the AI towards what I wanted, questioned its answers, directed the design and reviewed every text.

Claude (Anthropic) proposed the plan, collected the data from the filings, built the model, the note, the deck and this page, and helped me interpret the results. It also acted as a tutor: it explained what I had not yet covered in class and pointed me to methods I did not know.

An AI error the checks caught

A draft said On had outgrown Asics “over four years”. The data showed it only for 2024 and 2025; in H1 2026 Asics grew almost as fast. Corrected, the finding became part of the valuation argument.
